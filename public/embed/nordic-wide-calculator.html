Plan Terms — Summary

  • Investment amount depends on the selected plan.
  • All plans have a 12-month waiting period.
  • After 12 months, Nordic Wide covers Google Ads up to the selected daily budget limit.
  • Manager fee becomes 0 immediately after joining the plan.
  • Daily Google Ads coverage remains fixed at the selected plan level.
  • Benefits remain valid as long as the investment remains with Nordic Wide.
  • The calculator provides an estimate based on the selected assumptions.
  • Final contractual terms are governed by the signed agreement.

Refund policy — summary

  • If the investment is cancelled according to the applicable agreement, the refund is based on the selected plan investment amount.
  • Refunds are normally returned to the same bank account used for the original investment, subject to applicable verification and agreement terms.
